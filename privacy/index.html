MAKE A FACE Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Short version: your face never leaves your iPhone. MAKE A FACE has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. The only thing that ever leaves the app is a clip or picture that you choose to save or share.

Face data (ARKit face tracking)

MAKE A FACE uses Apple's ARKit face tracking (the TrueDepth camera, or the front camera on A12 and later) to read your facial expressions. For example, it reads how far your mouth is open, whether you blink, and how much you tilt or turn your head.

  • This face data is processed in real time, on your device, in memory. It is used only to control the game and to animate your cartoon character.
  • Face data is never stored, never sent off your device, and never shared with us or anyone else.
  • Face data is not used for advertising, marketing, analytics, profiling or identifying you, and it is not used to build any kind of face model.
  • We do not use the face mesh or depth data at all. We read a handful of expression values (such as "jaw open: 60%") 60 times a second and discard them.

Calibration

To work for every face, the game measures your resting face and your strongest version of each expression. It stores only the result: two numbers per expression (your resting value and your maximum) plus your resting head angle. These numbers stay in the app's private storage on your device. You can delete them at any time in Settings > Delete calibration data, or by deleting the app. They are never uploaded.

Party mode calibrations for other players are kept in memory only and are discarded when the party ends.

Stretch break measures how you're sitting at the start of each break so it can tell how far you tilt, turn and nod. That measurement is kept in memory only and discarded when the break ends. The app keeps only how many breaks you've done and your stretch streak.

Camera and clips

Each run is recorded so the game can show you a clip: your face (from the front camera) on top and the gameplay below.

  • While you play, the clip is written to the app's temporary storage on your device so it can play back the moment the run ends.
  • It is deleted automatically when you start another run, leave the results screen, or quit the app. Nothing is kept unless you tap Save or Share.
  • Save adds the clip, or the "Face of the run" picture, to your Photos library. The app asks for add-only access and never reads your library.
  • Share opens the iOS share sheet, and the clip goes only to the app or person you pick (for example TikTok, Instagram or WhatsApp). What happens next is governed by that service's privacy policy.

Microphone (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Microphone in clips in Settings, your microphone audio is mixed into your clips (screams and laughs welcome). Like the video, this audio stays on your device in temporary storage and is only kept if you save or share a clip. With the setting off, the app does not use the microphone at all.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, and the app can show your Game Center friends' scores. Apple processes this under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive it. No face data, images or audio are ever sent to Game Center. The game works fully without Game Center.

What we store on your device

  • Settings (sound, music, haptics, reduce motion, microphone preference)
  • Progress (best scores, distances, daily streak, unlocked characters and trails)
  • Calibration numbers (see above)

All of this lives only in the app's private storage and is removed when you delete the app.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not operate servers for this app, do not use third-party SDKs, and do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Children

MAKE A FACE does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and at engjellhas.github.io/makeaface/privacy, with a new effective date. Questions: engjell@vorex.co
