Which iPhones can play with face controls?

Face controls need an iPhone XR, XS or newer (a TrueDepth camera or an A12 Bionic chip or later). On other iPhones the game opens in Practice mode, played with on-screen touch buttons.

The game doesn't notice my faces

  • Hold the phone at eye level, about an arm's length away, with your whole face in view.
  • Play in good light and avoid a bright window behind you.
  • Redo the calibration: Settings › Recalibrate faces. Pull each face as big as you can while it's measured.

I said no to camera access

Open the iPhone Settings app › MAKE A FACE › turn on Camera. You can still play Practice mode without it: on the home screen tap Modes › Practice.

My clips don't save to Photos

The game asks only to add photos. If you declined, open the iPhone Settings app › MAKE A FACE › Photos and choose Add Photos Only.

Can my voice be in the clips?

Yes, if you want: turn on Settings › Microphone in clips. It's off by default, and the audio stays on your iPhone unless you save or share a clip.

Leaderboards and friends

Sign in to Game Center in the iPhone Settings app to see leaderboards and your friends' scores. The game works fully without Game Center.

Stretch break

A 90-second break with slow neck tilts, turns and nods, then a few big faces. Move gently, never bounce, and stop if anything hurts or you feel dizzy. It's a fun break, not medical advice.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the game keeps stays on your iPhone. Delete your face calibration in Settings › Delete calibration data, or delete the app to remove everything. See the privacy policy for details.
